I am working on an MLIR dialect for OpenSHMEM. Check that stuff out in the mlir directory. There's stuff in mlir/lib/dialect/OpenSHMEM, mlir/include/dialect/OpenSHMEM, mlir/tests/dialect/OpenSHMEM. The OpenSHMEM-specific transformations go in mlir/lib/dialect/OpenSHMEM/Transforms and mlir/include/dialect/OpenSHMEM/Transforms. The roadmap for the development in this dialect can be found in @roadmap.md . As we make additions/changes to this work, we will continuously update this file to track our progress. Scripts for compiling and running the OpenSHMEM MLIR dialect can be found in the `tmp` directory. Look at the existing code/implementation to determine where we are at.
